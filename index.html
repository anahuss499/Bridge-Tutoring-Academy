FAQs

Tutoring and childcare FAQs for Bradford parents


Who is Bridge for?

Children aged 8–16 who would benefit from school tuition or childcare.

When does it run?

We provide after-school and weekend sessions.

What will my child study?

Maths, English, Science, Reading, 11+, GCSE and SATs preparation, plus Homework, After-School and Holiday Clubs.

How do I register?

Complete one pre-enrolment form per child. Please do not include medical records, financial documents or benefit statements. You can also message Saqib directly with any questions.

Does submitting the form confirm a place?

No. Submitting the form does not confirm a place or commit you to payment. Our admissions team will contact you to discuss your child’s needs, available provision, sessions and fees. Childcare places are subject to availability and completion of the required registration arrangements.

How does the referral reward work?

If you recommend Bridge to a family who successfully joins our childcare provision, you can earn up to £150. Terms and conditions apply.
